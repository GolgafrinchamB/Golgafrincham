GOLGAFRINCHAM

a cargo hold of gloriously unnecessary work

Side projects that plainly did not need to exist, built carefully anyway. The thinkers stayed home. The doers stayed home. This ship carries everyone else — the telephone sanitisers, the hairdressers, the jingle writers and, per the official manifest, one (1) accountant — travelling steerage with their hobbies.

Live 3-D tracking of the complete public satellite catalog — sixteen thousand spacecraft propagated in your browser with SGP4, over a NASA Earth with a real day/night terminator. CARGO 002 Awaiting assignment SUSPENDED ANIMATION

Berth reserved. The Captain is in the bath.

Golgafrincham rid itself of a whole useless third of its population by loading them onto the B Ark with tales of impending doom — the mutant star goat, the twelve-foot piranha bees — and a promise that everyone else would follow shortly. Everyone else did not follow. Everyone else was later wiped out by a virulent disease contracted from a dirty telephone.
